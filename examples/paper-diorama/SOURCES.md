## Generation & Verification Notes
- Raw illustrations were synthesized via the Antigravity `image-generator` pipeline on pure `#FFFFFF` backgrounds (zero text/labels/watermarks) and converted losslessly to PNG before `@make-pictures/cli` Exterior Border Flood-Fill cutout processing.
- Temporary `raw/` folder was removed after QA verification.
- Every cutout was inspected individually on `#0F172A`/`#1E293B` dark checkerboards (`out/qa-diorama-cutout-*.png`) and together on the directory contact sheet (`out/qa-diorama-assets.png`): `holeCount: 0` across all 5 assets, crisp silhouettes, zero white halo fringe, and zero embedded raster text.
